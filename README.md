### 🔬 Overview

I work at the intersection of **computational Earth sciences, physical modeling, and artificial intelligence**. Currently **Remote Sensing Lead & Researcher** at [SIATA](https://siata.gov.co/) (Early Warning System of Medellín and the Aburrá Valley) and **Adjunct Professor** of Geotechnical Instrumentation at [Universidad EAFIT](https://www.eafit.edu.co/).

My work centers on designing operational, real-time early warning pipelines that fuse **physically-based numerical engines, weather radar grids, dense IoT telemetry, drone photogrammetry, and machine learning** to monitor and forecast landslides and debris flows across the tropical Andes.

* **M.Sc. Water Resources Engineering** — Universidad Nacional de Colombia (2022)
* **B.Sc. Geological Engineering** — Universidad Nacional de Colombia (2017)

### 🌐 Operational Impact & Research Scale

* **Multi-Hazard Early Warning Pipelines:** Operational hazard forecasting models deployed at SIATA covering the Aburrá Valley metropolitan region.
* **Sensor & Radar Integration:** Ingestion of dual weather radars (C-Band / X-Band) and real-time environmental IoT networks into predictive hazard models.
* **UAS & Remote Sensing:** Photogrammetric UAV surveys in steep Andean catchments for 3D terrain modeling and hazard zonation.
* **Academic Publications:** Peer-reviewed articles on slope stability, climate change IDF scenarios, and debris flows ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O0ef_-YAAAAJ&hl=en)).
